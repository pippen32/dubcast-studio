Sample — script before / after
Original · English · 0:00–0:12

“Okay, this changed everything. In the next sixty seconds, I’ll show you the mistake that’s quietly costing you money — and yeah, I made it too. Stick around, because tip number three is the one nobody talks about.”

Adapted · Spanish · 0:00–0:12

“Esto lo cambió todo. En los próximos sesenta segundos te muestro el error que te está costando dinero sin que te des cuenta — y sí, yo también caí en él. Quédate, porque el tercer consejo es el que nadie te cuenta.”

Adaptation note 1 — idiom changed“Quietly costing you money” is not translated word-for-word. It becomes “te está costando dinero sin que te des cuenta” — how a native speaker actually says it.
Adaptation note 2 — hook / joke kept alive“Stick around… the one nobody talks about” becomes “Quédate… el que nadie te cuenta.” Same curiosity hook, native rhythm — a literal translation would have killed it.
Adaptation note 3 — timed to the pictureThe Spanish line is re-cut to land inside the same 0:00–0:12 window, so the dub syncs with your original edit instead of drifting over it.
